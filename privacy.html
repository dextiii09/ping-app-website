Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Ping helps local businesses and creators find and work with each other. To do that we need some information about you. This page explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Ping is operated by ReachUp Media, India ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to letstalk@reachupmedia.in.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored securely by our authentication provider, never in plain text) and whether you joined as a creator or a brand. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from them.
  • Profile details you add: location, niche tags, bio, profile photo, company, job title, industry, company size, website, social media handles, follower and engagement figures, rate card or budget range, and any verification document link you submit.
  • Activity on Ping: who you ping or pass, your matches, Deal Room messages, Smart Proposals, campaign briefs and pitches, notifications, reports and blocks, and your settings (for example email notification preferences).
  • Technical information: basic device, browser and log data (such as IP address and timestamps) processed by our hosting and database providers to run and secure the service. We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in.

3. How we use it

  • To create and run your account and show your profile to other members.
  • To power discovery and matching by location and niche, and to calculate your PingScore.
  • To deliver messages, proposals, briefs, pitches and notifications, including emails if you have turned them on.
  • To keep Ping safe: verifying members, handling reports, and preventing spam, fraud and abuse.
  • To understand what works and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use advertising trackers.

4. What other members can see

Your public profile (name, photo, location, niches, bio, company, social handles, stats, rates and PingScore) is visible to other signed-in Ping members. Deal Room messages and proposals are visible only to the people in that match. Passes are private: nobody is told when you pass on them.

5. Service providers we share data with

We use trusted providers to run Ping. They process data on our behalf and only for the purposes below:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Google and Facebook: only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • OpenRouter and Google (Gemini): when you use Ping AI features, such as the bio writer or suggested messages, the text needed for that request is sent to an AI model to generate a response.
  • Cloudflare (Turnstile): when you sign up, log in or request a password or confirmation email, a bot check reads browser and device signals (such as your IP address) to tell people from automated programs.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): when you tap "Detect", your device's coordinates are sent to turn them into a place name. We only store the place name.
  • Resend: to send notification emails, if you have them turned on.
  • Content delivery networks (Google Fonts, jsDelivr, unpkg and Unsplash): our pages load fonts, code libraries and images from them, so they receive your IP address and browser information.

Some of these providers may process data outside India. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our members.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable time, except where we need to keep some of it to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent abuse. Messages you sent may remain visible to the people you sent them to.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit most of your profile inside Ping, and turn email notifications on or off in Settings. Under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance. Email letstalk@reachupmedia.in and we will respond as soon as we can.

Deleting your account and data: email letstalk@reachupmedia.in from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Ping account", and we will delete your account and personal data as described in section 6. If you signed in with Google or Facebook, you can also remove Ping from that account's connected apps; that stops the sign-in but does not delete your Ping data, so please email us as well.

8. Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and row-level security rules so members can only reach the data they are allowed to see. No service can be perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us straight away if you notice anything suspicious.

9. Age

Ping is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has signed up, please contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Ping grows. We will change the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app or by email.
